03 / The shortlist method

Use the sheet.
Read the details.

This Kakobuy spreadsheet guide turns a long list of links into a more useful comparison. Keep a few clear notes for every candidate, and leave unknown details marked as unknown.

When browsing Kakobuy finds, avoid treating a repeated recommendation or an attractive photo as independent proof of quality.

  1. 01

    Choose one category first

    Separate shoes, layers and accessories. Comparing similar items makes differences in fit, materials and price easier to spot.

  2. 02

    Save the exact variant

    Record the source URL, selected size, color and date checked. Different options on the same listing can have different prices or specifications.

  3. 03

    Separate claims from observations

    Write “seller states cotton” when the material is a seller claim. Use photos to inspect visible details without assuming they prove composition or authenticity.

  4. 04

    Compare the full estimated cost

    Check available delivery quotes, service fees and applicable charges. Keep estimates separate from confirmed amounts until checkout.

06 / Clear answers

Know what
you are opening.

A few practical answers before you follow a product link or add a new item to your shortlist.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a product-link list used to organize shopping research. Depending on its creator, it may include categories, prices, sizing notes and photo references. A spreadsheet entry does not verify quality, authenticity or current availability.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page is a browsing guide and category directory. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS; it does not offer an XLSX file, a Google Sheet or a live Kakobuy inventory feed.

How should I compare shoes and clothing?

For shoes, check the listed size system, available measurements and exact colorway. For clothing, compare garment measurements against something you already own. Confirm that photos and notes refer to the variant you intend to select.

Can QC photos prove that a branded item is authentic?

No. Quality-control photos may help you inspect visible details, but they do not by themselves establish authenticity, materials or long-term durability. Check provenance and use authorized retailers when authenticity is essential.

Is the listed product price the final cost?

Not necessarily. Depending on the transaction, the final amount may include domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, payment or currency-conversion costs, and applicable taxes or duties. Verify the charges before paying.

Is this an official Kakobuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent editorial guide linking to CAFANS. It does not claim affiliation with Kakobuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès. The illustrations are editorial graphics, not verified product listings.
